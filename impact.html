Healthcare Emergency

The 2:15 AM Highway Call That Saved Murugesan

When Murugesan suffered severe polytrauma after an accident on the deserted ECR highway, local bystanders called our 24/7 hotline. Within 8.5 minutes, our ambulance arrived with oxygen and paramedic stabilization. Today, Murugesan has returned to farming and supporting his two young daughters.

"Annai Foundation gave me a second chance at life. Without their free oxygen support, my daughters would have been left all alone."
Youth & Digital Career

From Financial Despair to Junior IT Executive

Priya, daughter of a daily-wage fisherman in Karaikal, had strong academic marks but lacked modern workplace software confidence. Through Annai Foundation's complimentary 'Grow with Google' boot camp, she mastered digital tools and interview skills, landing her first formal job.

"The Google certified workshop built my confidence to sit in corporate interviews without fear."
