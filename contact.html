Frequently Asked Questions

Are donations eligible for 80G tax exemption?
Yes! All contributions to Annai Foundation qualify for 50% income tax deduction under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act 1961. You can download your official tax receipt instantly.
How fast does the 24/7 ambulance respond?
Our ambulance operates round-the-clock across Karaikal with an average arrival time of 8.5 minutes within municipal limits, equipped with oxygen and first-aid.
How can my college organize a Google AI / Blood Camp?
You can select "Host a Camp" on the contact form. Our coordinator team will reach out with complete equipment, instructors, and certificates.
